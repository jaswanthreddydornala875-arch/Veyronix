Width

The width of a letter or numeral must be at least one third of its height, except for the numeral 1 and the letters i, I and l. Rule 7(3)

Panel area

Rectangular package: height × width of one side. Cylindrical: 40% of height × circumference. Other shapes: 40% of total surface area. Tops, bottoms, can flanges and bottle necks are excluded. Rule 7(4)

E-commerce listings

Every mandatory declaration except month and year of manufacture must appear on the platform before the sale is completed. Height rules do not apply to a digital listing. Rule 6(10)

Penalty on non-conforming packages

Fine up to ₹25,000 for a first offence, up to ₹50,000 for a second, and ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 or imprisonment up to one year, or both, for subsequent offences. Legal Metrology Act 2009, Sec. 36(1)
